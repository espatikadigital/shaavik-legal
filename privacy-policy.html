2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including:

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries;
  • Communicate with you regarding our services;

  • Protect our legitimate business interests;

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Depending on the circumstances, we process personal data on one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Your consent;
  • Our legitimate interests;
  • The performance of a contract or taking steps before entering into a contract;
  • Compliance with our legal obligations.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Where appropriate, we may share information with:

  • Professional advisers;
  • External service providers who assist in operating our business;
  • Regulated law firms where your matter requires reserved legal activities or solicitor-led representation;

We will only share information where it is lawful and appropriate to do so.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and to comply with applicable legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

Where an enquiry does not proceed, information will normally be retained only for an appropriate period unless a longer retention period is required by law.

7. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to safeguard personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
